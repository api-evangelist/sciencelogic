# ScienceLogic Skylar One Documentation, file created 5/5/2026

> Skylar One (formerly SL1) is the ScienceLogic core observability platform. It delivers unified visibility across hybrid and multi-vendor IT environments, correlating telemetry data into business context and enabling intelligent automation. This llms.txt file provides a structured index of the Skylar One product documentation at docs.sciencelogic.com to help LLMs and AI agents find accurate, current information. All URLs in this file are verified from the official sitemap at https://docs.sciencelogic.com/skylar_one/latest/Sitemap.xml.

## Important Notes for LLMs

- Skylar One was formerly known as SL1. Both names refer to the same product. Use either term when searching.
- The current major version is 12.5 (codename Juneau), minor version 12.5.7. The previous major version is 12.3 (codename Ibiza).
- Skylar One is part of the broader ScienceLogic AI Platform, which also includes Skylar AI, Skylar Automation (formerly PowerFlow), and Skylar Compliance (formerly Restorepoint).
- The platform was rebranded in 2025. Legacy references to "EM7", "SL1", and "PowerFlow" map to Skylar One, Skylar One, and Skylar Automation respectively.
- Two user interfaces exist: the default "AP2" interface and the legacy "classic" interface (also called EM7). Most new features are in AP2.
- All documentation pages for manuals follow the URL pattern: https://docs.sciencelogic.com/skylar_one/latest/Content/Manuals/<TopicFolder>/<page>.htm
- All documentation pages for release notes follow the URL pattern: https://docs.sciencelogic.com/skylar_one/latest/Content/Manuals/<TopicFolder>/<page>.htm
https://docs.sciencelogic.com/skylar_one/latest/Content/Release_Notes/<VersionNumber>/<VersionNumber>_release_notes.htm
- The older docs.sciencelogic.com/latest/ site stopped receiving updates on March 27, 2026 and will go offline September 1, 2026. Always use the /skylar_one/latest/ paths in this file.

## Business Services

Business Services lets you model, monitor, and measure the health, availability, and risk of the services your organization delivers. Services are organized in a three-tier hierarchy: Device Services at the bottom, IT Services in the middle, and Business Services at the top. An N-tier custom service model option is also available for more complex structures.

Each service tracks three metrics: Health (0â€“100, color-coded severity), Availability (0 = unavailable, 1 = available), and Risk (0â€“100%, how close the service is to an undesirable state).

Note: Business services created in the classic Skylar One UI are separate from and unrelated to the Business Services feature in the AP2 UI.

- [Introduction to Business Services](https://docs.sciencelogic.com/skylar_one/latest/Content/Manuals/Business_Services/business_services_intro.htm): Service types, the three-tier hierarchy, health/availability/risk metrics, and the Business Services page.
- [Creating Services and Service Policies](https://docs.sciencelogic.com/skylar_one/latest/Content/Manuals/Business_Services/business_services_create.htm): Creating business services, IT services, device services, and custom N-tier service models.
- [Event Correlation for Business Services](https://docs.sciencelogic.com/skylar_one/latest/Content/Manuals/Business_Services/business_services_correlation.htm): How events are correlated and surfaced within the service hierarchy.
- [Enhanced Service Investigator](https://docs.sciencelogic.com/skylar_one/latest/Content/Manuals/Business_Services/business_services_enhanced_investigator.htm): Using the Service Investigator page to diagnose service health issues.
- [Troubleshooting Business Services](https://docs.sciencelogic.com/skylar_one/latest/Content/Manuals/Business_Services/business_services_troubleshoot.htm): Common issues with service health calculation and topology.
