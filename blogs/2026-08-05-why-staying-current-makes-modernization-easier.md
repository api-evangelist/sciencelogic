Modernization Is Rarely a Single Project Most organizations don’t experience modernization as a single initiative. It unfolds over months and years, through a series of decisions made as technology shifts, business needs change, and operational demands grow. Teams adopt new capabilities, automate manual work, sharpen visibility, and strengthen security.
