What Customers Notice and What Engineering Prioritizes When organizations evaluate software platforms, attention naturally gravitates toward visible outcomes. New capabilities, expanded functionality, improved user experiences, and innovative technologies often dominate conversations about platform value. These improvements are important because they directly influence how teams interact with technology and how organizations achieve business objectives.
